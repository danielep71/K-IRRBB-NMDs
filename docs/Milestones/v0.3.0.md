# 📉 v0.3.0 — Decay model

[← Index](README.md) · [GitHub milestone](https://github.com/danielep71/VBA-IRRBB-Toolkit/milestone/3) · [Previous](v0.2.0.md) · [Next →](v0.4.0.md)

## 🎯 Purpose

Deliver a verified Decay engine in the Excel application. The model
describes the aggregate balance dynamics of a segment and currency,
producing minimum-probable-amount profiles, runoff, and mean life under
the accepted contract.

## 💡 Why it matters

Translating balances without contractual maturity into a time profile
requires explicit assumptions, observed variability, a horizon, and a
runoff allocation. A declining chart alone does not establish correctness:
amounts must reconcile to the opening balance, and statistics must be
compared with values produced independently of the VBA implementation.

## 🛠️ Activities and deliverables

1. **Review the prototype.** Inspect `test/decay-model`, its source,
   generators, examples, and expected values. Reuse only work that meets
   architecture, contract, and verification requirements; the experimental
   branch is not an already accepted application deliverable.
2. **Prepare the series.** Build aggregate balances separately by segment
   and currency, logarithmic changes, and declared structural breaks.
   Apply domain, continuity, and sample-size rules.
3. **Estimate and diagnose.** Estimate drift, break effects, and residual
   volatility. Record settings and contract-required diagnostics, making
   distributional assumptions explicit.
4. **Build the profile.** Calculate minimum probable amounts, runoff,
   terminal remainder, core/non-core measures, and mean life. Explain the
   conservative treatment of growth and allocation of the terminal remainder.
5. **Verify in Excel.** Integrate independent numerical cases and a repeatable
   worked example into the v0.2.0 test harness.

## 🔎 Practical example

For a synthetic monthly series, the user selects the window, segment,
currency, and supported settings. The output contains coefficients,
diagnostics, and a profile whose amounts can be reconstructed. Runoff plus
the terminal remainder must equal the opening balance within the stated
tolerance. The profile must satisfy the contract's properties in boundary
cases as well as ordinary cases.

The aggregate series includes new accounts. Its dynamics therefore do not
automatically represent survival of balances already present at the origin.
State this distinction with the results; it is central to later composition
with Stable.

## 🔗 Dependencies and interpretation

The [v0.2.0](v0.2.0.md) importer, data rules, and Excel tests are required,
along with relevant amendments and the sources needed for the method.
The Decay contract has no direct market-rate input: changing a rate scenario
does not authorize inventing an effect on the formula. Transmission between
models requires the v0.6.0 integration contract.

Statistical outputs remain separate from regulatory repricing constraints.
Any experimental overlay code must remain distinct: applicable overlays
are delivered in v0.7.0 after the necessary source verification.

## ✅ Completion criteria

- Coefficients, volatility, profiles, and mean lives match independent
  references within justified, recorded tolerances.
- Tests cover notional conservation, monotonicity, structural breaks,
  domain boundaries, insufficient samples, and failure paths.
- Generators and expected values have provenance, versions, and seeds where
  applicable; no expected value is copied from the output under test.
- Compilation, regressions, and the worked example run in Excel on the
  exact commit, with failures and cleanup documented.
- Code reaches the integration branch only after review, required checks,
  and the Excel evidence specified by governance.

## 🚧 Boundaries and tracking

LibreOffice execution is preliminary evidence, not Excel certification.
Numerical verification is completed here, rather than deferred to v0.9.0.
This milestone delivers neither a full EVE/NII engine nor a general claim
of regulatory compliance.
