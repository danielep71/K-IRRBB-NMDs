# 🏦 v0.5.0 — Stable-amount model

[← Index](README.md) · [GitHub milestone](https://github.com/danielep71/K-IRRBB_Behavioral_Models/milestone/5) · [Previous](v0.4.0.md) · [Next →](v0.6.0.md)

## 🎯 Objective

Estimate the survival of existing balances and stable amounts over multiple
periods, by segment and currency, with reproducible estimation, explicit
aggregation and failures that never leave valid-looking parameters.

**Why it matters.** An aggregate balance can remain constant because new
deposits offset outflows from existing accounts; that does not establish
that the opening balance remained available. Stable must distinguish
survival of existing amounts from growth and new inflows, and prevent
missing-data or weighting rules from creating artificial stability.

## 📦 Scope

- Resolve the Stable amendments from #26 in #34 before implementation:
  estimand, weights, aggregation, covariance, finite-sample adjustments,
  selection and boundary-case behavior.
- Build transitions under approved rules: balances, cash-out, verified
  closures, observation gaps, end of sample and migrations, with the
  transitions admitted to estimation observable.
- Prepare predictors using only information available at the forecast origin
  under #32, recording transformations and missing-value handling.
- Implement balance-weighted fractional logit, predictor selection and robust
  account-clustered covariance with conventions fixed before development,
  reusing reviewed common numerical primitives.
- Aggregate survival shares and amounts by account or cohort and by segment
  and currency, with explicit weight evolution and the relationship between
  opening amount, remaining share and runoff preserved.
- Build the registered cases `STAB-CASH-01`, `STAB-LOGIT-01`,
  `STAB-ROBUST-01`, `STAB-SEP-01`, `STAB-CONV-01` and `STAB-LEAK-01`, and the
  stable parts of `OOS-FREEZE-01`, `OOS-LEAK-01` and `OOS-SCORE-01`.

## 🏁 Exit criteria

- [ ] Coefficients and covariance match an independent external reference,
  with documented versions, settings and tolerances.
- [ ] Hand-constructed cases verify transitions, survival, weight evolution
  and multi-period aggregation.
- [ ] Leakage tests prevent future information from entering predictors and
  distinguish closure, missing data and end-of-sample censoring.
- [ ] Separation, non-convergence and singular systems fail with diagnostics,
  without saving valid-looking parameters or making a previous run's results
  appear current.
- [ ] Existing data-import, decay and rate results are unchanged.
- [ ] Excel compile, the regression harness, the new stable cases and failure
  paths run on Windows 64-bit Excel on the exact commit.
- [ ] README, CHANGELOG, `MODEL_CONTRACTS.md`, `TEST_CASES.md` and this guide
  distinguish survival of existing balances from aggregate growth or new
  accounts.

**Example acceptance case: why weights matter.** Two cohorts have opening
balances of 50 each and constant survival shares of 0.9 and 0.1. After two
periods, summing the individual cohorts gives `50 × 0.9² + 50 × 0.1² = 41`;
applying the initial average of 0.5 twice to the total gives
`100 × 0.5² = 25`. The two operations represent different quantities. This
case belongs to prerequisite #34, which selects and justifies the
aggregation; this guide does not approve an alternative formula in place of
that amendment.

## 🚫 Not in scope

Aggregate growth or new-account modeling (survival of existing balances is
not a forecast of total deposit growth), composition with Decay
([v0.6.0](v0.6.0.md), #37), regulatory constraints, and any external
numerical runtime in the workbook.
