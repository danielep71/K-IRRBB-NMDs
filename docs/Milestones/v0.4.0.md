# 📈 v0.4.0 — Interest-rate model

[← Index](README.md) · [GitHub milestone](https://github.com/danielep71/VBA-IRRBB-Toolkit/milestone/4) · [Previous](v0.3.0.md) · [Next →](v0.5.0.md)

## 🎯 Purpose

Estimate and simulate market-rate pass-through to customer rates. The
deliverable includes estimation, specification selection, diagnostics,
and forecast recursion by segment and currency, with verifiable results
and no external numerical runtime dependency in the workbook.

## 💡 Why it matters

Administered rates may respond with a lag and differently to increases
and decreases. Automatically assuming full pass-through or selecting a
regression solely for its fit can produce inconsistent simulations.
The model must distinguish the long-run relationship, short-run dynamics,
and admissibility, including cases where the data cannot support a valid fit.

## 🛠️ Activities and deliverables

| Component | Activity | Observable result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Data | Align customer and market series using contract-defined weights, dates, and populations | Traceable estimation sample and excluded observations |
| Primitives | Implement and verify the numerical operations and OLS regression required | Solutions compared with independent references |
| Specification | ADF and Engle–Granger cointegration tests; ECM/DIFF forms and asymmetry where allowed | Explained, reproducible model-form selection |
| Selection | Compare lags and models using comparable samples and deterministic rules | Search table, exclusions, and selection criterion |
| Uncertainty | Contract-defined HAC covariance and diagnostics | Statistics with stated conventions and limitations |
| Simulation | Recurse the customer rate along the selected market-rate path | Forecasts reconstructible from initial state and parameters |

Reject singular, unstable, or otherwise inadmissible candidates with explicit
diagnostics. Any simulation constraints remain distinct from estimated
coefficients: do not silently adjust an inadmissible fit to make it look valid.

## 🧩 Decisions required before implementation

Issue #33 requires a definition of full dynamic stability, including lagged
dependent variables, root orientation, and numerical tolerance. Bounds on
beta and lambda alone are insufficient. Equal-score AIC/BIC ties, comparable
samples, candidate ordering, HAC covariance, and ECM/DIFF diagnostics and reporting must also
be specified. This guide does not choose those details in place of the
authoritative contract.

## 🔎 Practical example

A synthetic series has a known customer/market relationship and dynamics.
Verify that estimation and simulation reproduce independent values within
the stated tolerances. A second case proposes unstable dynamics: it must
be rejected even if individual coefficients meet their bounds. Identical
data and settings must produce the same selection and explanation.

## 🔗 Dependencies

The [v0.2.0](v0.2.0.md) data workflow and test harness are required, together
with the accepted #33 amendment and verification of the necessary econometric
sources. Following Decay in the delivery sequence does not imply dependency
on its formula. Python or R may produce offline reference values, but must
not become a workbook runtime requirement.

## ✅ Completion criteria

- Independent references cover coefficients, statistics, covariance, lag
  selection, and forecasts.
- Stability, singularity, insufficient data, and inadmissible candidates
  are exercised with cases that distinguish correct acceptance and rejection.
- Settings, samples, ordering, and diagnostics make estimation repeatable
  and explainable.
- The workbook compiles and runs regressions and scenarios in Excel on
  the exact commit, including result invalidation and recovery after errors.
- Sources and the limits of statistical inference are documented with
  the model version.

## 🚧 Boundaries and tracking

This milestone delivers the Rates engine and its recursion. Orchestration
with Stable and Decay belongs to v0.6.0; the coordinated supervisory scenario
catalog belongs to v0.7.0. It does not calculate NII for the entire banking book.

[#33](https://github.com/danielep71/VBA-IRRBB-Toolkit/issues/33) tracks the
methodological prerequisite. Implementation and integration work must be
broken down into issues with expected evidence before development. Closing
the specification alone does not complete the engine.
