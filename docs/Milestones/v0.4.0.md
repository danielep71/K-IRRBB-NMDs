# 📈 v0.4.0 — Interest-rate model

[← Index](README.md) · [GitHub milestone](https://github.com/danielep71/K-IRRBB_Behavioral_Models/milestone/4) · [Previous](v0.3.0.md) · [Next →](v0.5.0.md)

## 🎯 Objective

Estimate and simulate market-rate pass-through to customer rates
reproducibly, by segment and currency: estimation, specification selection,
diagnostics and forecast recursion, with verifiable results and no external
numerical runtime in the workbook.

**Why it matters.** Administered rates may respond with a lag and differently
to increases and decreases. Assuming full pass-through, or selecting a
regression solely for its fit, can produce inconsistent simulations. The
model must distinguish the long-run relationship, short-run dynamics and
admissibility, including cases where the data cannot support a valid fit.

## 📦 Scope

- Resolve the Rates amendments from #26 in #33 before implementation: full
  dynamic stability (lagged dependent variables, root orientation, numerical
  tolerance; bounds on beta and lambda alone are insufficient), equal-score
  AIC/BIC ties, comparable samples, candidate ordering, HAC covariance, and
  ECM/DIFF diagnostics and reporting.
- Align customer and market series using contract-defined weights, dates and
  populations, with a traceable estimation sample and excluded observations.
- Implement and verify the required numerical primitives and OLS, reusing the
  reviewed `CORE_Math` primitives where they meet the contract.
- Specify the model: ADF and Engle–Granger cointegration tests, ECM/DIFF forms
  and asymmetry where allowed.
- Select lags and models on comparable samples with deterministic rules,
  publishing the search table, exclusions and selection criterion.
- Compute contract-defined HAC covariance and diagnostics, with stated
  conventions and limitations.
- Recurse the customer rate along the selected market-rate path; simulation
  constraints stay distinct from estimated coefficients.
- Build the registered cases `RATE-OLS-01`, `RATE-ADF-01`, `RATE-EG-01`,
  `RATE-ECM-01`, `RATE-ASYM-01`, `RATE-HAC-01`, `RATE-CONS-01` and
  `RATE-SIM-01`, and the rate parts of `OOS-FREEZE-01`, `OOS-LEAK-01` and
  `OOS-SCORE-01`.

## 🏁 Exit criteria

- [ ] Independent references cover coefficients, statistics, covariance, lag
  selection and forecasts within justified, recorded tolerances.
- [ ] Singular, unstable, insufficient-data and otherwise inadmissible
  candidates are rejected with explicit diagnostics, never silently adjusted
  to look valid; cases distinguish correct acceptance from correct rejection.
- [ ] Identical data and settings produce the same selection and explanation;
  samples, ordering and settings are recorded.
- [ ] Changed inputs or settings, and failed runs, invalidate prior results.
- [ ] Existing data-import and decay results are unchanged.
- [ ] Excel compile, the regression harness, the new rate cases, invalid-input
  and recovery cases run on Windows 64-bit Excel on the exact commit.
- [ ] README, CHANGELOG, `MODEL_CONTRACTS.md`, `SOURCES.md`, `TEST_CASES.md`
  and this guide document sources, the model version and the limits of
  statistical inference.

**Example acceptance case.** A synthetic series has a known customer/market
relationship and dynamics; estimation and simulation reproduce independent
values within the stated tolerances. A second case proposes unstable dynamics
and is rejected even though individual coefficients meet their bounds.
Identical data and settings produce the same selection and explanation.
