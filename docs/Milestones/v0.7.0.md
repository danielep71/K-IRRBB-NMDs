# ⚖️ v0.7.0 — Scenarios and regulatory overlays

[← Index](README.md) · [GitHub milestone](https://github.com/danielep71/VBA-IRRBB-Toolkit/milestone/7) · [Previous](v0.6.0.md) · [Next →](v0.8.0.md)

## 🎯 Purpose

Deliver consistent scenarios across models and a separate layer of
applicable regulatory constraints traceable to their sources. Results
must distinguish statistical estimation from the effects of a constraint
or scenario assumption.

## 💡 Why it matters

A duration estimated from data does not establish that the same duration
is permitted under a particular regulatory framework. Even a correctly
transcribed rule can be used in the wrong context. Identify its source,
version, applicability, and category mapping before applying it, and make
its effect on the result observable.

## 🛠️ Activities and deliverables

### 🌊 Coordinated scenarios

Define and record rate paths by currency, origin, and horizon, consistent
with the forms allowed by the contract. Transmit scenarios to Rates and
Stable through the dependencies accepted in v0.6.0. Running a scenario
does not give Decay a direct rate input that its specification does not have.

### 📚 Source verification and applicability

Complete #35 using official BIS, EBA, and EUR-Lex texts. Every implemented
rule must identify its document, version, exact location, access evidence,
and basis for applicability. Verify currency-specific parameters, any
floors, and the mapping between regulatory categories and data segments.
Unverified rules remain explicitly provisional or unsupported.

### 🧮 Constraints kept separate from estimation

Apply relevant constraints in a separate layer without rewriting calibrated
coefficients. Retain the statistical profile, constrained profile, and
reconciliation of the difference. Distinguish core/non-core amounts,
time allocation, and repricing assumptions under the selected framework.

## 🔎 Practical example

A synthetic case starts with a statistical profile and selects a framework
for which a particular rule has been verified and found applicable.
The output shows the original profile, transformation, final profile,
and reconciliation of amounts. If a required parameter remains unverified,
the feature must not present the outcome as validated or compliant.
Numerical values for the case must come from an independent reference.

## 🔗 Dependencies

The [v0.6.0](v0.6.0.md) integrated workflow, #35, and applicability decisions
are required before each affected overlay. Umbrella issue #26 collects
remaining amendments; its v0.7.0 allocation does not postpone #32–#34,
#36, or #37 beyond the implementations that depend on them.

## ✅ Completion criteria

- Every applied rule has an accessible official source, exact reference,
  version, and documented applicability; unsupported choices are identifiable.
- Scenarios, currency-specific parameters, and constraints have independent
  cases with recorded results and tolerances.
- The system retains separate statistical and constrained outputs and
  demonstrates their reconciliation without changing the original fit.
- Coordinated execution and failure cases are verified in Excel on the
  exact commit.
- The original obligations in #26 are reconciled with evidence; creating
  child issues alone does not complete them.

## 🚧 Boundaries and tracking

This guide does not independently verify or update regulations or numerical
limits. Their authoritative verification status is in the
[source register](../methodology/SOURCES.md). The milestone does not deliver
full banking-book EVE/NII calculations or authorize a universal compliance
claim for the toolkit.
